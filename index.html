FAQ

Is it really anonymous?

Using Tab Vault is: there's no account, no analytics and your tabs never leave your browser. Paying is not fully anonymous — our payment provider, Polar, needs an email and a card like any store, and we can see that email for receipts and refunds. That's all we can see, and it's never linked to your browsing because your browsing never reaches us.

What does the extension send over the network?

Free: nothing. Pro: once a day, your license key and a random device label (like "Tab Vault device 3fa91c") to Polar to check the license is valid. Never URLs, titles or sessions. Privacy policy.

Will I never lose tabs again?

No software can promise that. Tab Vault keeps several versions, a second copy in separate storage, optional backup files outside the browser and an alert when data suddenly drops.

What happens if I cancel the monthly plan?

Nothing is deleted. Tab Vault goes back to Free limits for future snapshots, and everything you already have stays.

How do I move from OneTab or Session Buddy?

Export from your current extension and import into Tab Vault — free in every plan. Migration guide.

Does it work in Firefox?

Yes. Tab groups are a Chrome feature and are skipped in Firefox; everything else works the same.
