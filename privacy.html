Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

Short version: Tab Vault keeps your data in your browser. It has no server, no account and no analytics. The only network request is the Pro license check, which contains your license key and nothing about your browsing.

What is stored, and where

  • Snapshots (URL, title, pinned state, tab group and window of your open tabs) and collections: in the extension's own browser storage (IndexedDB), plus a "last good copy" in the extension's local storage. They never leave your device.
  • Settings: extension local storage.
  • Backup files, only when you export or turn on automatic export: your Downloads folder (Tab Vault Backups/).
  • License key and activation id (Pro only): extension local storage.

Private/incognito windows are never saved. Browser-internal pages are ignored, and local files (file://) are ignored by default.

Network requests

Only one kind: when you activate Pro, and about once a day afterwards, Tab Vault sends to Polar (api.polar.sh, our payment provider and merchant of record) your license key, Tab Vault's organization id, an activation id and a label like Tab Vault · Chromium · 2026-10-12. It never sends URLs, titles, collections, snapshots or any browsing data. Free users make no network requests. Polar's privacy policy covers your purchase: polar.sh/legal/privacy.

What Tab Vault does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or trackers.
  • No remote code, external fonts or CDNs.
  • No access to page content (no host permissions).
  • No selling or sharing of data — we have none.

Deleting your data

Delete collections and snapshots in the Tab Vault panel, or uninstall the extension: the browser deletes all of its storage. Exported files are yours and stay until you delete them.
