Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

Short version: Tab Vault keeps your data in your browser. It has no server, no account and no analytics. The only network request is the Pro license check, which contains your license key and nothing about your browsing.

What is stored, and where

  • Snapshots (URL, title, pinned state, tab group and window of your open tabs) and collections: in the extension's own browser storage (IndexedDB), plus a "last good copy" in the extension's local storage. They never leave your device.
  • Settings: extension local storage.
  • Backup files, only when you export or turn on automatic export: your Downloads folder (Tab Vault Backups/).
  • License key and activation id (Pro only): extension local storage.

Private/incognito windows are never saved. Browser-internal pages are ignored, local files (file://) are ignored by default, sites you exclude are never written to disk, and tracking parameters (utm_*, fbclid, gclid…) are removed from links before saving. Encrypted backups use AES-256-GCM with a key derived from your passphrase; the passphrase is never stored.

Network requests

Only one kind: when you activate Pro, and about once a day afterwards, Tab Vault sends to Polar (api.polar.sh, our payment provider and merchant of record) your license key, Tab Vault's organization id, an activation id and a random device label such as Tab Vault device 3fa91c (no browser, OS or date). It never sends URLs, titles, collections, snapshots or any browsing data. Free users make no network requests at all.

Payments

Paying is the one moment we learn anything about you. Polar processes the payment and needs an email and a card, like any store; we can see the email and the plan you bought, for receipts, refunds and support. We never see your card. That email is never linked to your browsing, because your browsing never reaches us. Polar's privacy policy: polar.sh/legal/privacy.

Why Tab Vault is paid

Tools that are free usually make money from their users' data. We chose the opposite: Tab Vault never sees your data, so the only thing we sell is the software. You are the customer, not the product.

What Tab Vault does not do

  • No account, no login, no email required to use the extension.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or trackers.
  • No remote code, external fonts or CDNs.
  • No access to page content (no host permissions).
  • No selling or sharing of data — we have none.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all Tab Vault data wipes everything in one click; uninstalling the extension does the same. There is nothing to request from us: we hold no copy. Exported files are yours and stay until you delete them.
